Privacy Policy

Let's Hangout with Friends · Last updated: 16 October 2026

Let's Hangout with Friends ("the app", "we") lets you share photos, short-lived Moments, chat, join voice rooms and make voice or video calls with your friends. This page explains what information the app uses and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see your password in plain text).
  • Profile: profile photo, bio and privacy setting that you choose to add.
  • Content you create: photos and captions you post, Moments (removed after 24 hours), comments, likes, saved posts, chat messages and room activity.
  • Friends: your friend list, friend requests and users you block.
  • Calls and voice rooms: the app uses your microphone and, for video calls, your camera, to connect you live. Calls are not recorded by us.
  • Device and notifications: a push notification token for your phone so we can alert you about calls, messages and activity.
  • Reports: if you report a post, we store your report so we can review it.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your profile, posts, chats and friends.
  • To connect live calls and rooms.
  • To send notifications you asked for by installing the app and allowing notifications.
  • To keep the app safe, review reports and act on abuse.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take photos for posts and Moments and for video calls.
  • Microphone: for voice rooms, voice and video calls.
  • Photos / files: to choose a picture from your gallery.
  • Notifications: to alert you about calls, messages and activity.

Services we use

To provide the app we rely on trusted providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – sign-in, database and photo storage.
  • Agora – real-time voice and video for calls and rooms.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) – delivering push notifications.
  • Gmail (Google) – sending the verification code emails when you sign up or delete your account.

Who can see your content

Your posts and Moments are shown to your friends. Your profile may be visible to other users depending on your privacy setting. Chat messages are visible to you and the person you wrote to. You can block or report any user.

How long we keep it

  • Moments are removed automatically after 24 hours.
  • Like, comment and share alerts are removed after 24 hours.
  • Other data stays until you delete it or delete your account.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can delete individual posts and edit your profile at any time.
  • You can delete your account inside the app (Profile → Edit profile → Delete account). To protect you, we ask for your username, email and password and send a verification code to your email. Your account is then permanently deleted after 24 hours, including your profile, posts, Moments, messages and friends. If you log in again within those 24 hours you can choose to keep your account or continue with the deletion.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing us at the address below.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to the database is restricted by account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
